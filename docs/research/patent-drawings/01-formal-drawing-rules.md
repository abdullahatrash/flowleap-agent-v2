# Formal drawing rules for utility patent drawings (US, EP, PCT)

Research date: 2026-10-12. Scope: utility applications. Design applications are out of scope (section 7).
Primary sources only. Each row cites the rule and section. Verbatim quotes are in quotation marks.

## 1. Sheet size, margins, usable surface

| Item | USPTO 37 CFR 1.84(f),(g) | EPO A49 Art. 1(1), Art. 2(2); GL A-IX 4.1 | PCT Rule 11.5, 11.6(c) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheet size | A4 21.0 x 29.7 cm or Letter 21.6 x 27.9 cm (8.5 x 11 in). All sheets in one application the same size. "One of the shorter sides of the sheet is regarded as its top." | A4 only (29.7 x 21 cm), upright | A4 only (29.7 x 21 cm) |
| Top margin, min | 2.5 cm (1 in) | 2.5 cm | 2.5 cm |
| Left margin, min | 2.5 cm (1 in) | 2.5 cm | 2.5 cm |
| Right margin, min | 1.5 cm (5/8 in) | 1.5 cm | 1.5 cm |
| Bottom margin, min | 1.0 cm (3/8 in) | 1 cm | 1 cm |
| Usable surface ("sight"), max | A4: 17.0 x 26.2 cm. Letter: 17.6 x 24.4 cm (6 15/16 x 9 5/8 in) | 26.2 x 17 cm | 26.2 x 17.0 cm |
| Frames | "must not contain frames around the sight" | "shall not be surrounded by frames" | "shall not contain frames around the usable or used surface" |
| Scan targets | "should have scan target points (i.e., cross-hairs) printed on two catercorner margin corners" (should, not must) | none | none |
| Identification in top margin | 1.84(c): title, inventor name, application or docket number, "within the top margin" (optional) | none for drawings | Rule 11.6(e): file reference only, top-left, within 1.5 cm of top edge |
| One side only, no holes | 1.84(e), (x) | A49 Art. 2(1) | Rule 11.2 |

Program note: an A4 sheet with the four minimum margins gives exactly the 17.0 x 26.2 cm maximum in all three systems. Use A4 as the default and the three offices agree.

|---|---|---|---|

MPEP 507(E): OPAP objects if more than one figure and not labelled "Fig." with consecutive numerals. | "FIG" "whatever the official language", consecutive Arabic numerals, single figure not numbered and no "FIG", GL A-IX 5.2 | "Fig." in any language; single figure not numbered, IP 5.141; Rule 11.13(k) consecutive Arabic numerals |

Note on orientation: the US turns the sheet so the top is on the **right**; the EPO and PCT put the top of the figure on the **left**. A generator must flip the landscape rule per office.

## 7. Design patents (out of scope, key differences only)

Source: [37 CFR 1.152 as quoted in MPEP 1503.02](https://www.uspto.gov/web/offices/pac/mpep/s1503.html). Design drawings "must contain a sufficient number of views to constitute a complete disclosure of the appearance of the design" (there is no "seven views" rule in the text; the six orthogonal views plus a perspective are practice, not regulation). "Appropriate and adequate surface shading should be used to show the character or contour of the surfaces." Solid black shading is only for the colour black or colour contrast. Broken lines show environment or claim boundary and "may not be used to show hidden planes and surfaces". Colour drawings are permitted without a petition in design applications (1.84(a)(2)). OPAP does not review design drawings for publication (MPEP 507). Not covered by the planned generator.

## 8. Common objections and the form paragraphs used

No office publishes a ranked dataset of drawing objections. The USPTO lists what OPAP objects to on filing (MPEP 507), and what examiners object to later (MPEP 608.02(b), 608.02(e), 608.02(f)). The EPO Receiving Section checks only the A49 items listed in A49 Art. 4(2), "limited to the extent necessary for ... a satisfactory reproduction or a reasonably uniform publication" (Art. 4(3)); the formal defect is raised under Rule 57(i) and Rule 58 EPC with an invitation to correct (GL A-IX 10.1). Order below is by stage of review, not by frequency.

## 10. Rules only a human can judge

- "As many views as necessary to show the invention" and the choice of plan, elevation, section, perspective (1.84(h); GL A-IX 5; IP 5.136 to 5.138). A program can count views, not judge sufficiency.
- The drawing shows "every feature of the invention specified in the claims" (1.83(a); form paragraph 6.22.01). Needs claim reading against the geometry.
- Shading "aids in understanding" and is "not so extensive as to impede legibility" (1.84(m); GL A-IX 7.2).
- A difference in proportion is "indispensable for the clarity of the figure" (A49 Art. 1(2)(f)).
- Text is "absolutely indispensable" (Rule 11.11; A49 Art. 1(2)(j)); a flowchart step needs a sentence, not a word (GL A-IX 8).
- A photograph is "the only practicable medium" (1.84(b)); colour is "the only practical medium" (1.84(a)(2)).
- Conventional symbols are "universally recognized" and not confusable (1.84(n); GL A-IX 9).
- Legends are suitable (1.84(o)) and the chosen front-page figure is the most representative (1.84(j); GL A-IX 2.3).
- Modified or enlarged views are placed so the "relationship between the different parts" is "clear and unambiguous" (1.84(h)(2)(ii); GL A-IX 5.3).
- No new matter in replacement or colourised drawings (Art. 123(2) EPC, GL A-IX 10; 35 U.S.C. 132 via MPEP 608.02).
- No prohibited matter such as advertising or business marks (Rule 48(1)(c); GL A-IX 6).
