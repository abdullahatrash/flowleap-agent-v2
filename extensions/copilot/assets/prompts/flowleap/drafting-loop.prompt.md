This page is a guide, not a prompt. Read it once before your first matter.

An application with drawings is a loop. It runs over days, in one chat per matter.

```
disclosure
    |
Feature List . . . . click Confirm Feature List
    |
claims . . . . . . . click Approve Claims
    |
draft + findings . . click Waive Errors…
    |
figures  . . . . . . ask for a render; fix; ask again
    |
drawing set  . . . . ask for the build; read the receipt
    ^
    |  a claim change opens the approval again
    |  a figure change runs the coverage check again
    |  a new render of a Figure needs a new review cycle
```

You type three things:

- Start from a disclosure: attach it and ask for a US or EPO application with drawings.
- Remove or add a claim: say which claim, in plain words. The agent writes the claims again and asks for your approval.
- Render or build: ask for one Figure by its number, or ask for the drawing set when every Figure is done.

You click three buttons, at the top of the file:

- Confirm Feature List, on the Feature List, when every row is correct.
- Approve Claims, on the claims, after each change.
- Waive Errors…, on the findings, when a check is wrong and you accept the risk.

Stay in one chat for one matter. Start a new chat after you restart the app or after an update, and when the agent refuses the same thing twice.

Your matter is in the folder drafting/<matter>/. Open checklist.md there first: it says what is open and what you do next.
