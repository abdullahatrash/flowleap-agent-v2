Break each independent claim into its elements, map the dependency chain of the dependent claims, and assess the scope of each element against the description and the preferred embodiments. Support every reading with a verifiable quotation and a clickable source citation, and state clearly where the text is ambiguous. Save a structured claim-analysis report as outputs/claim-analysis.md in the workspace and give me a brief summary with a link to it.
