# UPC Representative — First Steps

Tell a lawyer or a European Patent Attorney if they may represent parties at the UPC, how to register in the court's filing system, and what to do when the Registrar finds a deficiency or rejects the request. This skill is about the person, not a case: for a court filing see upc-filing-prep, for what can be filed next see upc-case-navigator.

The entitlement routes, the evidence, the Registrar outcomes, the review procedure, removal and re-entry, the account roles and the screen labels are in [references/registration.md](references/registration.md).

## Step 1 — Find the entitlement route
- **Lawyer** authorised to practise before a court of a Member State: Art. 48(1) UPCA; proof is a certificate under R.286.1 RoP.
- **European Patent Attorney**: Art. 48(2) UPCA, only with **appropriate qualifications** (R.286.2 RoP):
  - the European Patent Litigation Certificate (R.13 EPLC Rules), or
  - a bachelor or master degree in law, or an equivalent state exam in law, of an EU Member State (R.11 EPLC Rules).
  - The listed courses and the three-national-cases route (R.12 EPLC Rules) were open only for one year from the entry into force of the UPCA. That window has closed. Say so when the user relies on it.
- Without a route, a patent attorney can still **assist** a representative (Art. 48(4) UPCA), but cannot sign or be named as representative.

## Step 2 — Give the registration steps and the evidence
The request goes to the Registrar from the user's **own account**: complete the profile, open "Manage representative registration", select the entitlement under "Representative justification", upload the evidence as PDF (a copy of the diploma, certificate or other proof, R.14 EPLC Rules), then "Send verification". No court fee. Each person registers on their own account, not the firm.

## Step 3 — Explain the Registrar's outcomes
- **Entered on the list** (R.15.2 EPLC Rules): the user can be named as representative and can sign filings.
- **Deficiency** (R.15.4): a formal defect; correct it within a **non-extendable period of two months**, or the request is rejected.
- **Rejection** (R.15.3): a **petition for review** with the Registrar, in an EPO official language, **within one month of notification** of the decision, with the reasons (R.18). The Registrar can rectify (R.19.1); otherwise the President of the Court of Appeal decides (R.19.2, R.20). The screen offers a time-limit extension request, but R.18 provides none: file in time.

## Step 4 — Cover removal, re-entry and team roles when asked
- Removal on request (R.16.4), strike-off when no longer a European Patent Attorney or no longer qualified (R.16.2, R.16.3), re-entry by a new request; a strike-off can be reviewed (R.17). Before removal, arrange a replacement in active cases (R.293 RoP).
- Roles: **drafter** and **payer** need no registration; only a **registered representative** can be named as representative and **sign** a court filing (opt-out filings excepted, R.5.3(b) RoP). A firm uses an organisation account whose administrator invites members and assigns roles.

## Step 5 — Output
Give the route with its article and rule, the evidence to upload, the screen steps, and the outcomes with their periods. Give each period with its trigger event ("one month of notification of the decision"). Never give a calendar date.
