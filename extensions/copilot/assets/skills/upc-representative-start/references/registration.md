# UPC Representative Registration — Reference

**Sources (checked 2026-10-08):** Article 48 UPC Agreement; Rules 286 and 293 of the Rules of Procedure, consolidated text at https://www.unifiedpatentcourt.org/sites/default/files/upc_documents/Consolidated%20Rules%20of%20Procedure%20UPC_EN.pdf; Rules on the European Patent Litigation Certificate and other appropriate qualifications pursuant to Article 48(2) UPCA ("EPLC Rules"), Administrative Committee decision AC/06/22022022, at https://www.unified-patent-court.org/sites/default/files/ac_06_22022022_rev_e_0.pdf; screen labels as the court's filing system shows them on the user profile. **Caveat:** the UPC Agreement, the Rules of Procedure, the EPLC Rules and the UPC Registry govern. Treat this file as an analysis aid, not authority, and verify against the official sources before acting.

## Who may represent (Art. 48 UPCA)

| Route | Who | Proof (R.286 RoP) |
|-------|-----|-------------------|
| Art. 48(1) | A lawyer authorised to practise before a court of a Member State | A certificate that they are a lawyer authorised to practise before a court of an EU Member State (R.286.1). In later actions they may refer to the certificate already lodged |
| Art. 48(2) | A European Patent Attorney entitled to act before the EPO **who has appropriate qualifications** | The European Patent Litigation Certificate, or other justification of appropriate qualifications (R.286.2) |

- Parties to a UPC Case must be represented by such a representative (Art. 48(1)–(2)). An opt-out request needs no representative (R.5.3(b) RoP).
- A patent attorney without the qualifications can still **assist** a representative and may speak at hearings, subject to the Rules of Procedure (Art. 48(4)). They do not sign as representative.
- A change of representative in a case follows R.293 RoP; the new representative files their own proof.

## Appropriate qualifications for a European Patent Attorney (EPLC Rules)

| Rule | Qualification | Status |
|------|---------------|--------|
| R.2–R.10, R.13 | **European Patent Litigation Certificate** from an accredited course (universities, non-profit educational bodies, the Training Centre). Lodged with the Registrar; the attorney is then entered on the list of entitled representatives (R.13; Art. 48(3)) | Open |
| R.11 | A **bachelor or master degree in law** under the educational standards of an EU Member State, or a passed **equivalent state exam in law** of an EU Member State | Open |
| R.12.1(a) | Completion of one of the listed courses or certificates (for example the CEIPI diplomas, the FernUniversität in Hagen course "Law for Patent Attorneys", Queen Mary, Nottingham, IPReg certificates) | **Transitional only**: one year from the entry into force of the UPCA. That period has ended; the Court of Appeal holds that it is as a rule not extended |
| R.12.1(b) | Having represented a party without a lawyer, or acted as a judge, in at least three patent infringement actions before a national court of a Contracting Member State within the five years before the application | **Transitional only**, same one-year period; ended |

Tell a user who relies on a listed course (R.12.1(a)) or on prior cases (R.12.1(b)) that the window has closed: today the routes are the EPLC (R.13) or a law degree or state exam (R.11). A registration made under R.12 during the window stays permanent (R.16.1).

## The registration request

**The law.** The request for recognition is filed with the Registrar in an official language of the EPO (English, French or German) (R.14.1). For R.11 (and R.12.1(a)) it contains a **copy of the diploma, certificate or other proof** (R.14.2). For R.12.1(b) it identifies each infringement action (parties, court, date of commencement) with reasonably available evidence, such as the power of attorney (R.14.3). An EPLC holder lodges the Certificate (R.13). A lawyer lodges the R.286.1 certificate.

**The screens.** The request is made from the user's **own account** in the court's filing system:

1. Complete the user profile (email, first name, last name and a residence address). An incomplete profile blocks signing later.
2. Open the user profile and select **"Manage representative registration"** (page "Representative registration").
3. Under **"Representative justification"**, select one entitlement (the labels begin as follows):
   - "Article 48(1), Lawyer authorized to practice in contracting Member States"
   - "Article 48(2), Patent Attorney with EPLC"
   - "Article 48(2), Patent Attorney with other appropriate qualifications" (the R.11 and R.12 routes)
4. Upload the evidence as **PDF** files (one or more). The form does not say which document proves which entitlement: upload the proof that matches the selected entitlement (the bar admission or court certificate, the EPLC, the law diploma or state-exam certificate), plus a short cover letter that names the rule relied on.
5. Click **"Send verification"** and confirm. The request cannot be withdrawn from the screen after sending. While it is pending, more supporting documents can be uploaded.

No court fee is charged for registration.

## Registrar examination (R.15 EPLC Rules)

| Outcome | When | What the user does |
|---------|------|--------------------|
| Entered on the list | The R.11/R.12 requirements are met and the request complies with R.14 (R.15.2) | Nothing. The user can now be selected as representative in filings and can sign them |
| Deficiency found | The request fails a formal requirement of R.14 (R.15.4) | Correct the deficiencies within a **non-extendable period of two months**; on the screen, upload the corrected evidence and send the verification again. If not corrected in time, the request is rejected |
| Rejected | The request complies with R.14 but the R.11/R.12 requirements are not met (R.15.3) | Petition for review (below). The reasons are in the decision document on the user profile |

The Registrar may consult the Advisory Committee (R.15.1).

## Petition for review (R.17–R.20 EPLC Rules)

- **What can be challenged:** a decision of the Registrar under R.15 (the request) or R.16 (strike-off, re-entry) (R.17).
- **Form and period (R.18):** in writing, with the Registrar, in an official language of the EPO, **within one month of the notification of the challenged decision**, stating the reasons for setting the decision aside. Give the period and its trigger; the user computes the date.
- **Registrar revision (R.19):** if the petition is admissible and well founded, the Registrar rectifies the decision. If the Registrar does not rectify within one month of receipt, the petition goes to the **President of the Court of Appeal**.
- **President of the Court of Appeal (R.20):** if the petition is admissible and allowable, the President alters the Registrar's decision; otherwise rejects it. The President may ask the petitioner for comments.
- **The screens:** on the user profile, section **"Petition for review"**, "Select type of request" = "Petition for review", upload the petition as PDF, click **"Submit petition for review"**. When comments are requested, upload them and click **"Submit petition comments"**.
- **Form limit:** the same section also offers **"Request for extension of the time limit"**. R.18 provides no extension. File the petition itself within the one month; do not count on an extension.

## Removal and re-entry (R.16 EPLC Rules)

| Event | Rule | Effect |
|-------|------|--------|
| Removal on request | R.16.4 | The Registrar strikes the name off the list. On the screen: **"Request removal"**, upload a signed removal request (PDF), **"Send removal request"**. The Registrar may ask for comments first. Arrange a replacement representative in every active case before (R.293 RoP) |
| No longer a European Patent Attorney on the EPO list | R.16.2 | Registration ceases; the Registrar strikes the name off, on request or ex officio |
| Requirements not met or no longer met; registration obtained by fraud | R.16.3 | The Registrar strikes the name off |
| Re-entry on the EPO list after R.16.2 | R.16.2 | Re-entered on request |
| Re-entry after a removal | — | On the screen: **"Re-entry request"**: select the entitlement, upload a signed re-entry request and the evidence, **"Send re-entry request"**. The Registrar examines it as a new registration |

Otherwise a registration is permanent (R.16.1). A strike-off decision can be challenged by a petition for review (R.17).

## Accounts and team roles

| Role on the screens | Does | Must be a registered representative? |
|---------------------|------|--------------------------------------|
| Drafter | Prepares a filing | No |
| Signer | Signs a filing | **Yes**: the filing system lets only a registered representative sign a court filing. Opt-out filings are the exception (they need no representative, R.5.3(b) RoP). An unregistered user sees a warning before signing |
| Payer | Pays the court fee | No |
| Representative | Is named for a party in the filing; selected from registered representatives only | Yes |

- Each user has an individual account. A law firm can set up an **organisation**; its administrator invites members and assigns their roles. A drafter can invite another user to sign a submission.
- Registration attaches to the **person**, not to the firm: each lawyer or attorney who signs or is named as representative registers on their own account.
- When a representative is added to a case, the case goes into their individual portfolio; it can be moved to the organisation account.
