# UPC Opt-Out Request Types — Reference

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10. Derived from a study of the Unified Patent Court case-management system's opt-out request model plus the UPC Agreement (Art. 83) and Rule 5 of the Rules of Procedure. **Caveat:** the UPC Agreement, the Rules of Procedure, and the public UPC Registry govern; treat this as an analysis aid, not authority, and verify against the official sources before acting.

## The five request types

| # | Request type | Effect | Reversible? |
|---|--------------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Initial opt-out (Rule 5 RoP) | Removes a classic bundle EP from UPC jurisdiction for the life of the patent | Yes — by withdrawal (type 2), once |
| 2 | Withdrawal of opt-out (opt back in) | Returns the patent to UPC jurisdiction | No — a second opt-out is barred after withdrawal |
| 3 | Correction of an opt-out | Fixes errors in a filed opt-out without withdrawing it | n/a (does not change jurisdiction) |
| 4 | Removal of an unauthorized opt-out | Deletes an opt-out entered without proprietor authority | n/a |
| 5 | Removal of an unauthorized withdrawal | Reverses a withdrawal entered without authority | n/a |

## Requirements for an opt-out

- **All proprietors of all national parts** must join the opt-out. The proprietor set is drawn from every designated state of the bundle; a single missing co-proprietor makes the opt-out ineffective.
- A **declaration of proprietorship** accompanies the request.
- **No fee** is charged.
- **Representation:** a registered European Patent Attorney or UPC representative is not required to lodge an opt-out, but is commonly used.

## Timing rules (lock-in / lock-out)

| Rule | Trigger | Consequence |
|------|---------|-------------|
| No opt-out after a UPC action | A UPC action is brought on the patent | The opt-out option is foreclosed |
| No withdrawal after a national action | A national-court action is brought on the patent | The withdrawal (opt back in) option is foreclosed |
| No re-opt-out after withdrawal | An opt-out has been withdrawn | The patent stays under UPC jurisdiction; it cannot be opted out again |

## Duration

An opt-out is effective for the entire life of the patent unless and until it is withdrawn.
