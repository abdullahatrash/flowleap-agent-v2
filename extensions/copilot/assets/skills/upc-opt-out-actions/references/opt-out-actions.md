# UPC Opt-Out Request Types — Reference

**Sources (checked 2026-10-08):** Article 83 UPC Agreement; Rules 5 and 5A of the Rules of Procedure, consolidated text at https://www.unifiedpatentcourt.org/sites/default/files/upc_documents/Consolidated%20Rules%20of%20Procedure%20UPC_EN.pdf (index: https://www.unifiedpatentcourt.org/en/court/legal-documents); screen labels as the opt-out filing screens show them. **Caveat:** the UPC Agreement, the Rules of Procedure and the public UPC Registry govern. Treat this file as an analysis aid, not authority, and verify against the official sources before acting.

## The five request types

| # | Request type | Rule | Effect | Reversible? |
|---|--------------|------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Opt-out | R.5.1 | Takes a classic bundle EP or a published EP application out of the exclusive competence of the UPC, from the date of entry in the register (R.5.5) | Yes — by withdrawal (type 2), once |
| 2 | Withdrawal of an opt-out | R.5.7 | Returns the patent to UPC competence from the date of entry in the register | No — no further opt-out after the withdrawal is entered (R.5.10) |
| 3 | Correction — screen label "Opt-out/withdrawal correction" | R.5.5 | Corrects missing or incorrect particulars recorded in the register. The date of the correction is noted; the opt-out is effective from that date | n/a |
| 4 | Removal of an unauthorized opt-out | R.5A | Entry is first marked as subject to an Application for removal; deleted only on a final decision to remove | n/a |
| 5 | Removal of an unauthorized withdrawal | R.5A | Same procedure for a withdrawal | n/a |

## Who lodges an opt-out or a withdrawal

| Point | The law | The filing form |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Co-proprietors | All proprietors or applicants lodge the opt-out (R.5.1(a)); the same applies to a withdrawal (R.5.7, applying R.5.1(a)) | Accepts **one** proprietor only |
| States covered | All states for which the patent was granted or designated (R.5.1(b)); a withdrawal cannot be split by state (R.5.7) | — |
| SPC holder | Joins the request if different from the proprietor (R.5.2(a)) | — |
| Proprietor not in the register | Lodges a declaration of entitlement under R.8.5 (R.5.1(a), R.5.3(e)) | — |
| Representation | Not required; a representative or another person with a mandate may lodge it (R.5.3(b)) | — |
| Fee | None | — |

What to tell the user when there are co-proprietors: the law requires all of them; the form holds one. Get the authority of every co-proprietor before filing, and ask the Registry how to record the others (for example in an attached statement that names each one with its declaration). An opt-out that lacks a co-proprietor risks being ineffective or removed.

## Removal of an unauthorized opt-out or withdrawal (R.5A)

- **Who:** the proprietor, the applicant, or the SPC holder concerned, setting out the reasons (R.5A.1).
- **First effect:** the Registrar marks the entry as subject to an Application for removal (R.5A.2). The register shows it as pending removal. It is **not** removed yet.
- **Decision:** the Registrar decides as soon as practicable. Only a final decision to remove deletes the entry (R.5A.2).
- **Review:** an Application for review to the President of the Court of Appeal, in English, French or German, within one month of notification of the decision (R.5A.3).

## Timing rules (lock-in / lock-out)

| Rule | Trigger | Consequence |
|------|---------|-------------|
| R.5.6 | A UPC action commenced on the patent before the opt-out (or its correction) is entered | The opt-out is ineffective, whether the action is pending or concluded |
| R.5.8 | A national action commenced on the patent before the withdrawal is entered | The withdrawal is ineffective, whether the action is pending or concluded |
| R.5.10 | A withdrawal has been entered in the register | No further opt-out of that patent or application |
| R.5.9 | An opted-out application proceeds to grant as a European patent with unitary effect | The opt-out is deemed withdrawn; the Registrar enters the withdrawal |

## Duration

An opt-out stays effective for the life of the patent (including after expiry) unless it is withdrawn, deemed withdrawn (R.5.9) or removed (R.5A).
