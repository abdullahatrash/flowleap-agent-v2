# Exemplar rule

**start_application_draft** returns up to five exemplars from the `style/`
folder of the workspace: earlier applications of this client, chosen by the
attorney. The workspace is one client, so the exemplars are that client's.

## What an exemplar gives

- Voice: sentence length, person, the firm's usual phrasing.
- Structure: how sections open, how embodiments are introduced, how figures
  are walked through.
- Boilerplate wording, for example the closing paragraph on equivalents.
  Paragraphs taken this way are marked `model-proposed`.

## What an exemplar never gives

An exemplar is never a source. No fact, feature, embodiment, material, value,
example or result enters the draft from an exemplar, even when it fits the
invention. A technical sentence carries a `feature`, `disclosure` or
`instruction` marker, never a pointer to an exemplar. When an exemplar shows
an embodiment the disclosure lacks, that is at most an Inventor Question.

## When the folder is empty

Use the office template only. Never take exemplars from another workspace,
another matter of another client, or from published patents.
