# Pre-Filing Requirements — Reference

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10. Derived from a study of EPO front-office filing-validation behaviour plus general EPC/PCT knowledge. **Caveat:** official EPC/PCT rules, the EPO Guidelines, and the current fee schedules govern; treat everything below as a checklist aid, not authority. Verify each rule against the official source before relying on it for a real filing.

## Minimum parts for a filing date

| Part | Needed for filing date? | Notes |
|------|------------------------|-------|
| Description | Yes | Absence blocks a filing date. |
| Claims | No (may follow) | Absence opens a deficiency workflow with a correction deadline. |
| Abstract | No (may follow) | Same deficiency workflow as missing claims. |

## Deadline windows

| Situation | Window | Runs from | Format / dependency |
|-----------|--------|-----------|---------------------|
| Fresh filing claiming Paris priority | 12 months | Earliest priority date | Missing it forfeits the priority claim (restoration is exceptional). |
| PCT national/regional-phase entry | 30 months | Priority date | Source PCT number must be `PCT/CCyyyy/nnnnnn`. |
| Divisional filing | While parent pending | — | Requires the parent application number. |

## Applicant & representative constraints

- At least one applicant is mandatory.
- A lone applicant who is a US national generally must appoint a confirmed professional representative before the EPO.
- Only one applicant may use a correspondence address that differs from the others.
- Stated ownership shares must total 100%.

## Sequence listing (WIPO Standard ST.26)

- The requirement is triggered by **attaching a sequence listing**, not by the subject matter claimed.
- Consequence: an application disclosing nucleotide/amino-acid sequences should include a compliant ST.26 listing proactively; the filer, not the portal's subject-matter guess, is responsible for including it.

## Fee posture options

One of: already paid (with proof attached) · paid immediately at filing · deferred. This reference does not carry fee amounts — see the fee-reduction-advisor and excess-claims-estimator skills, and always confirm against the current official fee schedule.
