# PCT vs Direct-EP Routing — Reference

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10. Derived from a study of WIPO ePCT and EPO front-office validation behaviour plus general PCT/EPC knowledge. **Caveat:** the PCT, its Regulations, the EPO Guidelines, and the current fee schedules govern — verify timelines and fees against the official sources before relying on them.

## Where validation happens

| Route | Validator | What it checks | Where errors surface |
|-------|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| PCT via ePCT | WIPO's ePCT platform | Form fields, priority claims, designated states, fee calculation | Mostly up-front at WIPO; the receiving office's own checks are thin, so anything ePCT misses can lie dormant until national/regional phase |
| Direct EP | The EPO | Full formalities + fee validation, locally | Early, at the EPO — the office that will examine the case |

Key asymmetry: on the PCT route the **receiving office is not a meaningful backstop** — WIPO's validation is the safety net. On direct EP, the examining office validates at filing.

## Route-choice factors

| Factor | PCT | Direct EP |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| Time before committing to phases | ~30 months from priority | Commit now |
| Cost timing | Defers the multi-country spend; adds PCT fees | Pays sooner; no PCT fees |
| Geographic breadth | One filing preserves rights across many states | Europe only |
| Speed to European grant | Slower (adds the international phase) | Faster when Europe is the sole target |

## How to advise

Anchor the recommendation to the user's target markets and timeline: PCT when they need time and breadth or aren't yet sure where to nationalise; direct EP when Europe is the only target and speed/cost-now is preferred. Present amounts and exact deadlines as "verify against the current official schedule/rules".
