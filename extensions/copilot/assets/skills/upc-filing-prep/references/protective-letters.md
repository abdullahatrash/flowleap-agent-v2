# UPC Protective Letters (RoP 207) — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced: RoP 370), UPC Agreement (UPCA), official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It never gives a calendar date.

A protective letter is an Initial Filing. It is a stand-alone record, not a UPC Case: it has no opposing party until an Application for provisional measures arrives. It gets a "PL number".

## Protective letter

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | A person entitled to start proceedings under Art. 47 UPCA who considers it likely that an Application for provisional measures will be lodged against him as defendant in the near future (RoP 207.1). |
| Preconditions | None. No pending case is necessary. Where: the Registry, not a division (RoP 207.2). Screen: no division field. |
| Language | The language of the patent (RoP 207.2). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 207.2) | (a) the name of each defendant filing the letter and of the defendant's representative; (b) the name of the presumed applicant; (c) postal and electronic addresses for service on the filing defendant, and the persons authorised to accept service; (d) addresses for service on the presumed applicant, where available; (e) the patent number, where available, and any prior or pending proceedings (RoP 13.1(h)); (f) the statement that the letter is a Protective letter. |
| Optional content (RoP 207.3) | The facts, including a challenge to the facts the presumed applicant may rely on, and any assertion that the patent is invalid with its grounds; the written evidence; the arguments of law, including why an Application for provisional measures should be rejected. |
| Screen fields | Patent number (optional, EP + 7 digits); "Information about any prior or pending proceedings"; "Confidentiality of documents" (declare documents that must stay confidential in a later provisional-measures case); mandatory check box "I confirm this is a protective letter."; parties: defendant, defendant representative, "Presumed applicant for provisional measures" (at least one of each). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Protective Letter" (mandatory); "Exhibit Claimant"; "Other"; proof of fee reduction (if claimed); "Proof of payment" (if already paid). |
| Signing | A representative of the filing defendant. |
| Fee | RoP 207.4 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (protective letter). |
| Deficiencies | Correct within 14 days of service of the Registry's notice, and pay the fee where applicable (RoP 207.6). Screen: the "Correct deficiencies" filing. |
| Form vs law | Law: the patent number "where available" (RoP 207.2(e)); the rule sets no limit of one patent. Form: at most one patent number per letter. If several patents are at risk, plan one letter (and one fee) per patent. |

## Validity and confidentiality

- **Validity (RoP 207.9):** if no Application for provisional measures is lodged within 6 months from the date of receipt of the letter, the letter is removed from the register. The filer can prevent this with an extension (below).
- **Confidentiality (RoP 207.7–.8):** the letter is not public on the register. When an Application for provisional measures is lodged, the Registrar forwards the letter to the panel or judge and then to the applicant. On the screens, only the filer's side sees the letter in the list of protective letters. In a later provisional-measures case, the parties see only the letters that the court considers.
- **Effect:** the Court takes a relevant protective letter into account and in particular considers an oral hearing (RoP 209.2(d)). An applicant may withdraw its application when a protective letter exists (RoP 194.6, 209.5).

## Extension of a Protective letter (Subsequent Filing)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The person who lodged the protective letter (RoP 207.9). |
| Preconditions | The letter is still on the register: apply **before** the 6-month period expires. Screen: the "Extension of a Protective letter" filing is offered only until the end of the valid-until day, Luxembourg time. |
| Effect | A further 6 months. Further extensions are possible on further payment of the fee (RoP 207.9). Screen: the new period runs from the current valid-until date. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Extension of a Protective Letter" (mandatory); "Other"; proof of fee reduction (if claimed). |
| Fee | RoP 207.9 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (extension of a protective letter). |
| Time limit | RoP 207.9: before expiry of the 6-month period that runs from the date of receipt of the letter (or of the current extension). |

## What the screens do not offer

- No withdrawal filing for a protective letter. **Not verified:** the RoP have no express withdrawal rule for protective letters; ask the Registry.
- No applications, court communications or appeals on a protective-letter record. Only "Extension of a Protective letter" and "Correct deficiencies".
