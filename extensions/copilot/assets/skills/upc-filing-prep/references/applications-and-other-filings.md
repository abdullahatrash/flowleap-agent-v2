# UPC Applications, Other Filings and Court Communications — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced: RoP 70, 354.4, 370), UPC Agreement (UPCA), official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP, the UPCA and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It never gives a calendar date.

Signing for every filing here: on the screens, only a registered UPC representative can sign; one signature is enough (RoP 4.1, 8.1; Art. 48 UPCA).

## 1. "Application" inside a UPC Case

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Where | From inside an existing case: request type "Application by Rule / Article", "Response to Application by Rule / Article" or "Other Application". Not offered on opt-out records, protective letters or actions against EPO decisions. |
| Screen | Select one or more application types; each type is labelled with its rule (for example "RoP 333 - Application to review a case management Order"). Attach the application document for your side; an "Other Application" takes a document of your side. Each selected type that has a fee adds it. |
| Who may file | A party to the case, unless the rule names a side. |
| Case-opening types | "RoP 80", "RoP 126" and "RoP 151" applications each open their own UPC Case (separate proceedings: RoP 125, 150). |

Main application types, with their law:

| Application (screen label starts with) | Who | Rule, period, trigger | Fee (`fees-2026.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| RoP 9.3 - extend / shorten a time period | Any party | RoP 9.3: reasoned request; the Court cannot extend the RoP 198.1, 213.1 and 224.1 periods (RoP 9.4) | None |
| RoP 30.2 / RoP 50.2 - leave to further amend the patent | Proprietor | RoP 30.2, 50.2: only with the Court's permission | None |
| RoP 80 - compensation for licences | A party to the licence of right (RoP 80 does not name the side; not verified) | RoP 80.1 content: RoP 13.1(a)–(d), the Art. 8(1) Regulation 1257/2012 statement, the licence agreement. No RoP period | §1 + §2 |
| RoP 126 - determination of damages | Successful party | RoP 126: no later than 1 year from service of the final decision on the merits on infringement and validity (incl. appeal); content RoP 131 | §1 + §2 (RoP 132) |

## 2. "Other filings" (new record, at first instance or at the Court of Appeal)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Screen fields | "Filing type" (mandatory); "Court instance" (Court of First Instance or Court of Appeal); court division; language; patent numbers (EP + 7 digits); "Related case number" (optional); "Anticipated value" (mandatory only for types with a value-based fee); parties: claimant/applicant/appellant (mandatory), one representative, defendant/respondent, proprietor. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | At least one of "Statement of claim", "Application", "Exhibit", "Other"; for an intervention "Response to invitation to intervene"; proof of fee reduction and "Proof of payment" when a fee applies. |
| Follow-ups on the screen | "Statement of defence", "Reply to the Statement of Defence", "Rejoinder to the Reply to the Statement of Defence"; court communications; applications. |

Filing types and their law:

| Filing type (screen label) | Law | Who may file | Period | Fee (`fees-2026.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Declaration of non-infringement (Art. 32(1)(b)) | RoP 61–70. Central division (Art. 33(4) UPCA), unless an Art. 33(7) agreement. Condition: the proprietor or licensee asserted infringement, or did not give a written acknowledgment within 1 month of a written request with full particulars (RoP 61.1). Content: RoP 63. | The person doing or proposing the act (RoP 61.1); action against the proprietor or licensee (RoP 61.2) | None to start. Defence: RoP 67, 2 months from service of the Statement. Reply: RoP 69.1, 1 month. Rejoinder: RoP 69.2, 1 month from service of the Reply. Preliminary objection: RoP 66 with RoP 19. | §1 + §2 (RoP 70) |
| Compensation for licences (Art. 32(1)(h)) | RoP 80 (see section 1) | A party to the licence of right (not verified) | None | §1 + §2 |
| Damages (Art. 32(1)(a)) / Damages / compensation (Art. 32(1)(f)) | RoP 125–144. Compensation for provisional protection (Art. 32(1)(f) UPCA, Art. 67 EPC) is part of the damages determination (RoP 125). | Successful party | RoP 126: 1 year from service of the final decision on the merits | §1 + §2 (RoP 132). **Not verified:** the table has no separate line for a stand-alone Art. 32(1)(f) action |
| Compensation for use (Art. 32(1)(g)) | Use of the invention before grant, or right based on prior use | — | None | §1 + §2 (RoP 70 as replaced applies the non-infringement fee to these actions) |
| Cost decisions (RoP150) | RoP 150–151 | Successful party | RoP 151: 1 month from service of the decision | None in the 2026 table |
| RoP 223 / RoP 223.4 - suspensive effect | See `appeal.md` | — | — | §4 |
| RoP 245 - Application for re-hearing | Art. 81 UPCA; RoP 245–250; at the Court of Appeal. Content: RoP 246 | Party adversely affected by a final decision (CFI decision only when the appeal period has expired) | RoP 245.2: 2 months from discovery of the fundamental procedural defect (or from the criminal finding), or from service of the final decision if later; never later than 10 years from service | §4 (RoP 250; the Court may waive it) |
| RoP 313 / 316 / 316A - Application to intervene | RoP 313: legal interest; in support of one party; content RoP 313.4 | The intervener, represented under Art. 48 UPCA (RoP 313.3) | RoP 313.2: before the closure of the written procedure, unless the Court orders otherwise | None |
| RoP 293 - change a representative; RoP 262.1(b) - access to the file; RoP 378 / 381 legal aid | As labelled | Party (RoP 262.1(b): member of the public) | — | None |

**Form vs law:** for "Compensation for use" the screen computes no fee. The law (RoP 70 as replaced, RoP 370.2(c)) charges the non-infringement fixed fee and, where applicable, the value-based fee. Expect the Registry to ask for it; budget it. For "Declaration of non-infringement" the screen offers every division; the law requires the central division unless Art. 33(7) applies.

## 3. Court communications (inside a case)

| Filing (screen title) | Use | Rule and period | Attachments |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communication to the Court | A communication that is not a pleading | No RoP period. Copy the other party (RoP 8.3) | One document for your side |
| Reply to order of the Court / Submission RoP 264 | Answer to an order, or the RoP 264 opportunity to be heard | The period that the order sets (RoP 9.1, 264) | Exhibit, document and submission for your side |
| Other filing | A filing not covered by another screen; also the "Statement in intervention" | The period set by the rule or the order | Depends on the case type and your side |

All three: no fee. Select your "filing capacity" first (for example Claimant, Defendant or Intervener); the attachment list follows it.

## 4. Correct deficiencies

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Use | After a deficiency notice of the Registry, in any court case or protective letter. |
| Screen | "Correct deficiencies": case number, filing capacity (mandatory), at least one document of your side. |
| Period | The notice states it. The RoP periods: 14 days of service of the notice for the Statement of claim (RoP 16.3), the Statement for revocation (RoP 47.1), the defence with a counterclaim (RoP 27.2), the action against an EPO decision (RoP 89.2), the protective letter (RoP 207.6) and the Statement of appeal (RoP 229.2). Missing it can lead to a decision by default (RoP 355, 357). |
| Fee | None for the correction. An unpaid court fee must be paid in the same period (RoP 16.3(b), 27.2(b), 229.2(b)). |
