## How to state a fee

- Take every amount from this file only. Give its rule and the table version ("2026 Table of Court Fees, in force from 1 January 2026").
- Give the fixed fee and say whether a value-based fee applies (§2).
- For a small or micro-enterprise, apply RoP 370.8 (§5): state the reduced amount (50%) and the condition (an affirmation, lodged with the filing, that the party is a small or micro-enterprise under Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC).
- For an action filed before 1 January 2026, tell the user to use the previous (2023) table and give no 2026 amount for it (see "Which table applies").
- Where the fee rule applies RoP 15.2, say that the filing is not deemed lodged until the fee is paid (see "Payment").

## Which table applies

- Actions and applications **filed after 31 December 2025** use this table (decision D-AC/08/02072025, Art. 5).
- Actions filed **before 2026-01-01** keep the previous table (adopted 8 July 2022; the "2023 table", in use when the Court started work). This file does not give those amounts. Use the previous official table.
- **Not verified:** how the Court treats a fee that becomes due after 2025 in an action filed before 2026 (for example a later application inside the old action). Ask the Registry.

## §5 Reduction and reimbursement (RoP 370.8–370.11)

| Rule | What applies |
|---|---|
| RoP 370.8 — small and micro-enterprises | Pay 50% of the regular fees of RoP 370.2–370.5. Condition: with the Statement of claim, counterclaim, application or appeal, lodge an electronic notification in the language of proceedings that affirms the party is a "small enterprise" or "micro-enterprise" under Title I of the Annex to Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC. The Court may ask for documents, and may order the rest of the fee (or the rest plus 50% if the affirmation is wrong); non-payment leads to a decision by default (RoP 355). |
| RoP 370.9(b) — withdrawal | 50% reimbursement if the action is withdrawn (RoP 265) before the closure of the written procedure. |
| RoP 370.9(c) — settlement at the Centre | 65% reimbursement if the action ends by settlement at the Patent Mediation and Arbitration Centre, or by its arbitral award, before the closure of the interim procedure (or the date the judge-rapporteur set at the interim conference). |
| RoP 370.9(d)–(e) | One reimbursement per action and party (the larger); the Court may deny or reduce it. |
| RoP 370.10 | Full or partial reimbursement if the fees threaten the economic existence of a party that is not a natural person. |
| RoP 370.11 | Lodge a reasoned Application for reimbursement. |

**Screen facts:** the party flag is "Entitled to 50% reduction in court fees", with the attachment "Proof of entitlement to 50% reduction in court fees". **Form vs law:** the screen offers the flag only for a party that is a legal entity; the law looks at enterprise size under Recommendation 2003/361/EC, which can include a self-employed natural person (not verified for the UPC). If your client is a natural person who qualifies, raise it with the Registry.

## Payment (screen facts and law)

- Payment method: "Already paid" (attach "Proof of payment") or "Pay later". The screen gives a payment reference; use it in the bank transfer.
- Law: the Statement of claim is not deemed lodged until the fee is paid (RoP 15.2). The same applies where a fee rule applies RoP 15.2 mutatis mutandis (for example RoP 26, 46, 53, 88.3, 192.5, 206.5, 207.4, 228, 250, 333.3). If the Registry finds the fee unpaid, it gives 14 days of service of its notice (RoP 16.3(b), 27.2(b), 229.2(b)).
