# UPC Action Against Decisions of the EPO (RoP 85–98) — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced: RoP 370), UPC Agreement (UPCA), Regulation (EU) No 1257/2012, official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP, the UPCA and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It never gives a calendar date.

Scope: decisions of the EPO in its tasks under Art. 9 of Regulation (EU) No 1257/2012 (unitary patent protection), Art. 32(1)(i) UPCA. The EPO is the defendant; the screen adds it automatically. The action is heard by the central division (RoP 92). Costs: each party bears its own (RoP 98).

## Two legal bases (screen field "legal basis", mandatory)

| Screen value | Application | Who may file | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| "RoP88 - Application to annul or alter a decision of the Office" | Any decision of the Office under Art. 9 of Regulation 1257/2012 | Any person adversely affected by the decision (Art. 47(7) UPCA) | RoP 88.1: 2 months from service of the decision of the Office |
| "RoP97 - Application to annul a decision of the Office to reject a request for unitary effect" | Expedited action against the rejection of a request for unitary effect | The proprietor whose request was rejected (RoP 97.1) | RoP 97.1: 3 weeks from service of the decision of the EPO |

## Application to annul or alter a decision of the Office (RoP 88)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Language | The language in which the patent was granted (RoP 88.1). Screen: English, French or German. |
| Mandatory content (RoP 88.2) | (a) claimant name and, where applicable, representative; (b) if the claimant is not the proprietor or applicant: explanation and evidence that it is adversely affected and entitled (Art. 47(7) UPCA); (c) addresses for service; (d) a reference to the contested decision; (e) prior or pending proceedings; (f) whether a single judge shall hear the action; (g) the order or remedy sought; (h) the grounds under RoP 87 (breach of Regulations 1257/2012 or 1260/2012, breach of the EPO implementing rules, breach of an essential procedural requirement, misuse of power); (i) facts, evidence and arguments; (j) list of documents with translation / RoP 262.2 / 262A requests. |
| Effect | The action has suspensive effect (RoP 86). |
| Fee | RoP 88.3 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (action against a decision of the EPO). |
| Deficiencies | Correct and pay within 14 days of service of the notice (RoP 89.2). |

## Application to annul a rejection of unitary effect (RoP 97)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Language | The language in which the patent was granted (RoP 97.1). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 97.2) | The particulars of RoP 88.2(a), (c), (d) and (g)–(j). |
| Procedure | The standing judge decides within 3 weeks of receipt (RoP 97.4). RoP 85, 88 (except as RoP 97.2 says), 89 and 91–96 do not apply (RoP 85.2). |
| Fee | RoP 97.2 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (same fee as RoP 88). |

## Common screen facts (both legal bases)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Screen fields | Patent numbers (EP + 7 digits; unitary patents allowed); legal basis; court division (a central division seat); language of proceedings; parties: claimant (mandatory), claimant representative (at most one), proprietor (optional); the EPO as defendant (added by the screen). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Application" (mandatory); "Exhibit Claimant"; "Document Claimant"; "Proof of proprietorship/adverse affect (RoP 88.2(b))"; proof of fee reduction (if claimed); "Proof of payment". |
| Signing | Screen: only a registered UPC representative can sign. |
| Form vs law | Law: representation is **not** required in these proceedings (Art. 48(7) UPCA; RoP 88.4: RoP 8 does not apply). Form: the filing must be signed by a registered UPC representative. A claimant without a representative cannot file through the screens; tell the user. |

## Subsequent Filings in the case

| Step | Side | Rule, period, trigger | Screen request type and mandatory attachment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interlocutory revision | EPO | RoP 91.1: within 2 months of receipt of the application, the EPO may rectify the decision | "Interlocutory revision" |
| Further written pleadings | Claimant | RoP 93.1: only on invitation of the judge-rapporteur, within the period specified | Request type for RoP 93.1 further written pleadings (exact label not verified); "Further pleadings" |
| Comments of the President of the EPO | EPO | RoP 94 (RoP 88 actions) or RoP 97.4 (RoP 97 actions): on invitation | "Comments by the President of the EPO (RoP 94)" / "(RoP 97.4)" |
| Observations on the President's comments | Claimant | RoP 94: the claimant may submit observations; period as the Court sets | Request type for observations on the President's comments (exact label not verified); "Observations" |

During the interim procedure, the judge-rapporteur asks the claimant whether it wants an oral hearing (RoP 95).

The usual "Application" filing (applications under a rule) is not offered in this case type; court communications, replies to orders, other filings and "Correct deficiencies" are offered.

## Appeal

| Decision | Who | Rule, period, trigger | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision in a RoP 88 action | Party adversely affected | RoP 220.1(a), 224.1(a): 2 months from service; grounds RoP 224.2(a): 4 months | `fees-2026.md` §4 (appeal in an action against an EPO decision) |
| Decision of the standing judge in a RoP 97 action | Proprietor or President of the EPO | RoP 97.5: 3 weeks from service of the decision; content: the RoP 97.2 particulars and the reasons for setting the decision aside | `fees-2026.md` §4 (same line) |

Screen: "Type of appeal" = "RoP 97.5" for a RoP 97 decision. "Anticipated value" is not required for these appeals.
