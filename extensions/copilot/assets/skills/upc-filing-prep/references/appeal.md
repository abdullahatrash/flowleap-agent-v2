# UPC Appeal, Appeal Against Orders, Cross-Appeal and Suspensive Effect — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced: RoP 223.1, 234, 354.4, 370), UPC Agreement (UPCA), official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP, the UPCA and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It never gives a calendar date.

## Common to every filing in this family

- Court of Appeal, Luxembourg. **Representation:** Art. 48 UPCA (RoP 8.1); for appeals in actions against EPO decisions see `action-against-epo-decisions.md`.
- **Signing:** on the screens, only a registered UPC representative can sign; one signature is enough.
- **Language** (RoP 227): the language of proceedings at first instance, or the language of grant if the parties agree under Art. 50(2) UPCA (lodge the respondent's agreement with the Statement of appeal).
- **Periods:** the Court cannot extend the RoP 224.1 periods (RoP 9.4). Grounds not raised in the RoP 224.2 period are inadmissible (RoP 233.3).

## What can be appealed (RoP 220–221)

| Decision or order | Route | Statement of appeal (RoP 224.1) | Grounds (RoP 224.2) | Screen "Type of appeal" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Final decision; decision ending proceedings for one party (RoP 220.1(a), (b)) | Appeal as of right | 2 months from service of the decision | 4 months from service of the decision | "RoP 220.1 (a) or (b)" |
| Order under Art. 62 UPCA (provisional measures) (RoP 220.1(c)) | Appeal as of right | 15 days from service of the order | 15 days from service of the order | "RoP 220.1(c), as to applications for provisional measures (Art. 62 UPCA)" |
| Order under Art. 60 or 61 UPCA (evidence, inspection, freezing) | Appeal as of right | 15 days | 15 days | "RoP 220.1(c) as to an application for orders referred to in Art. 60 or 61 UPCA" |
| Order under Art. 49(5), 59 or 67 UPCA (language change, production of evidence, information) | Appeal as of right | 15 days | 15 days | "RoP 220.1(c) as to an application for orders referred to in Art. 49.5, 59 or 67 UPCA" |
| Other orders (RoP 220.2) | With the final-decision appeal, or with leave of the Court of First Instance | 15 days from service of the decision granting leave | 15 days | "RoP 220.2" |
| Refusal of leave (RoP 220.3) | Request for discretionary review to the Court of Appeal | 15 calendar days from the end of the 15-day period that follows the order (RoP 220.3) | — | "RoP 220.3 - Request for discretionary review" |
| Cost decision (RoP 157, 221) | Application for leave to the standing judge | Application for leave: 15 days from service of the cost decision (RoP 221.1) | — | "RoP 221 - Application for leave to appeal a cost decision" |
| Decision in an action against an EPO decision | See `action-against-epo-decisions.md` | RoP 220.1(a), (b); for RoP 97: 3 weeks (RoP 97.5) | — | "RoP 97.5" |

A decision allowing a Preliminary objection is appealable under RoP 220.1(a); an order rejecting it only under RoP 220.2 (RoP 21.1). RoP 220.2 gives two routes: (1) together with the appeal against the final decision, or (2) with leave of the Court of First Instance, within 15 days from service of the decision granting leave (RoP 224.1(b), 224.2(b)). If leave is refused, a request for discretionary review is possible (RoP 220.3). Always state both routes.

## Statement of appeal (Initial Filing at the Court of Appeal)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | A party adversely affected (RoP 220.1). |
| Preconditions | A served decision or order of the Court of First Instance; leave where RoP 220.2 or 221 requires it. Screen: start the appeal from inside the first-instance case and select at least one issued order or decision. The screen does not offer the appeal while the first-instance case is still in its formalities or service stage. |
| Mandatory content (RoP 225) | (a) appellant and representative names; (b) respondent and representative names; (c) addresses for service and authorised persons; (d) the date of the decision or order and the first-instance action number; (e) the order or remedy sought, including any request for expedition (RoP 9.3(b)) with reasons. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of Appeal" or "Statement of appeal and statement of the grounds of appeal" (one is mandatory); "Statement of the Grounds of Appeal"; "Exhibit Appellant"; "Document Appellant"; proof of fee reduction (if claimed); "Proof of payment". |
| Other screen fields | "Type of appeal" (mandatory); optional applications (for example RoP 223); check box "Includes a request for suspensive effect (standard)"; "Anticipated value" (mandatory, except for appeals in actions against EPO decisions). |
| Fee | RoP 228 → `fees-2026.md` §4 (by type of appealed action). |
| Deficiencies | RoP 229.2: correct and pay within 14 days of service of the notice. An appeal outside the RoP 224.1 period is rejected as inadmissible (RoP 229.5). |
| Time limit | RoP 224.1, see the table above. |

## Statement of the grounds of appeal (Subsequent Filing)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The appellant. |
| Mandatory content (RoP 226) | (a) the parts of the decision or order contested; (b) the reasons for setting it aside; (c) the facts and evidence relied on (RoP 222.1–.2: new requests, facts and evidence may be disregarded). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of the Grounds of Appeal" (mandatory); "Document Appellant". |
| Fee | None. |
| Time limit | RoP 224.2: 4 months from service of the decision (RoP 220.1(a), (b)); 15 days from service of the order or of the RoP 220.2 / 221.3 decision. |

## Statement of response and cross-appeal (Subsequent Filing)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | Any other party to the first-instance proceedings (the respondent) (RoP 235). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 236) | (a) respondent and representative names; (b) addresses for service; (c) the appeal action number; (d) a response to the grounds of appeal. The respondent may support the decision on other grounds (RoP 236.2). |
| Cross-appeal (RoP 237) | Only inside the Statement of response, within its period. It must meet RoP 225 and 226. It is treated as an appeal for the fee (RoP 237.4). It falls if the appeal is withdrawn (RoP 237.5). Screen: tick "Includes Statement of Cross-Appeal". |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of Response" or "Statement of Response including Statement of cross-appeal" (one is mandatory); "Statement of Cross-Appeal" (if cross-appeal); one exhibit slot and one document slot for the respondent (exact labels not verified); proof of fee reduction; "Proof of payment". |
| Fee | None for the response. Cross-appeal: the appeal fee → `fees-2026.md` §4. |
| Time limit | RoP 235.1: 3 months from service of the grounds of appeal (decisions); RoP 235.2: 15 days from service of the grounds (orders). No response: the Court may decide (RoP 235.3). |

## Reply to the Statement of cross-appeal

Appellant. RoP 238.1: 2 months from service of the cross-appeal (decision appeals); RoP 238.2: 15 days (order appeals). Content: a response to the cross-appeal grounds. No fee. Screen: "Reply to the Statement of cross-appeal".

## Application for suspensive effect (RoP 223; Art. 74 UPCA)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Effect without an application | An appeal has no suspensive effect, unless the Court of Appeal decides otherwise (Art. 74(1) UPCA). An appeal against a decision on a revocation action or counterclaim, or on an Art. 32(1)(i) action, always has suspensive effect (Art. 74(2)). No suspensive effect for appeals under RoP 220.2, 220.3 or 221.3 (RoP 223.5). |
| Who may file | A party (RoP 223.1). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 223.2) | Why the appeal shall have suspensive effect; the facts, evidence and arguments. |
| Extreme urgency (RoP 223.4) | At any time, without formality, to the standing judge; the judge may require a written application later. Screen: a separate request type "RoP 223.4 - Application for suspensive effect with extreme urgency". |
| Screen | Check box "Includes a request for suspensive effect (standard)" in the Statement of appeal, or the application type "RoP 223 - Request for suspensive effect (standard)". |
| Fee | RoP 223.1 → `fees-2026.md` §4 (suspensive effect). **Not verified:** whether the same fee applies to an RoP 223.4 request. |
| Time limit | None in the RoP; the Court of Appeal decides without delay (RoP 223.3). |

## Challenge to the rejection of an appeal as inadmissible (RoP 234)

Appellant; within 1 month of service of the decision under RoP 224.1 or 233.2; no new grounds. Fee → `fees-2026.md` §4. Screen: application type "RoP 234 - Challenge to the decision to reject an appeal as inadmissible".
