# UPC Revocation Action and Application to Amend the Patent — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced), UPC Agreement (UPCA), EPC, official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP, the UPCA and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It gives the rule, the period and the trigger event, never a calendar date (service rules RoP 270–279 and RoP 300–301 move the real date).

## Common to every filing in this family

- **Representation:** Art. 48 UPCA (RoP 8.1). **Lodging and signing:** electronic, signed (RoP 4.1). On the screens, only a registered UPC representative can sign; one signature is enough ("Signature" image or typed text).
- **Fees:** amounts only from `fees-2026.md`.
- **Order of filings:** the screens offer every follow-up filing to every party at any time. The law sets the side and the order → `next-filings.md`.
- The counterclaim for revocation (inside a Statement of defence) and the counterclaim for infringement (inside a Defence to revocation) are in `infringement.md`.

## Statement for revocation (Initial Filing; Art. 32(1)(d) UPCA)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | Any person concerned by the patent (Art. 47(6) UPCA). The action is directed against the proprietor (RoP 42.1). |
| Preconditions | **Division:** the central division (Art. 33(4) UPCA). Exceptions: if an infringement action between the same parties on the same patent is already at a local or regional division, only that division (Art. 33(4)); or a local or regional division by agreement of the parties (Art. 33(7)). → `upc-division-router`. |
| Language | The language in which the patent was granted (RoP 45.1). With an Art. 33(7) agreement: a language under RoP 14.1 (RoP 45.2). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 44) | (a) particulars of RoP 13.1(a)–(d), (g), (h); (b) the agreed local or regional division with evidence of agreement, if Art. 33(7); (c) single judge with agreement, if applicable; (d) extent of revocation requested; (e) one or more grounds, with arguments of law and claim construction; (f) facts; (g) evidence; (h) orders sought in the interim procedure; (i) list of documents with translation / RoP 262.2 / 262A requests. RoP 13.2–.3 apply. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of claim" (mandatory; the screen uses this label for the Statement for revocation); "Exhibit Claimant"; "Document Claimant"; "Proof of entitlement to 50% reduction in court fees" (if claimed); "Proof of payment" (if "Already paid"). |
| Other screen fields | Patent numbers (EP + 7 digits); court division; language of proceedings; parties: claimant, claimant representative, defendant (mandatory), proprietor (optional); SPC data (optional). No value field. |
| Signing | A representative of the claimant. |
| Fee | RoP 46 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (fixed fee only). Not lodged until paid (RoP 46 with RoP 15.2). |
| Time limit | None to start. Deficiencies: 14 days of service of the notice (RoP 47.1 with RoP 16.3). |
| Form vs law | Law: central division unless Art. 33(4) second sentence or 33(7) applies. Form: the division list offers every division, and the language list follows the division chosen. Choose by the law. |

## Preliminary objection (RoP 48)

RoP 19.1–.3 and .5–.7 apply. Defendant; 1 month from service of the Statement for revocation; no fee in the 2026 table. Screen: the "Preliminary objection" filing (see `infringement.md`).

## Defence to revocation (Subsequent Filing; RoP 49–50)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The defendant (proprietor). |
| Mandatory content | RoP 24(a)–(c); RoP 29A(a)–(d) and (f) apply (facts, evidence, why the action fails including independent validity of dependent claims, interim orders, list of documents) (RoP 50.1). |
| May include | An Application to amend the patent (RoP 49.2(a)); a Counterclaim for infringement (RoP 49.2(b); see `infringement.md`). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of defence" (mandatory); "Application to Amend Patent"; "Counterclaim for infringement" (mandatory if "Includes counterclaim for infringement" is ticked); "Exhibit Defendant"; "Further pleadings"; "Document Defendant". |
| Fee | None, unless a counterclaim for infringement is included (RoP 53). |
| Time limit | RoP 49.1: 2 months from service of the Statement for revocation. |

## Application to amend the patent (RoP 30, 50.2)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The proprietor: in the Defence to revocation (RoP 49.2(a)), or in the Defence to a Counterclaim for revocation (RoP 30.1, 29(a)). |
| Mandatory content | (a) the proposed amendments of claims and/or description, with any auxiliary requests, in the language of grant; a translation into the language of proceedings if different, and for a unitary patent a further translation on the defendant's request (RoP 30.1(a)); (b) why the amendments meet Art. 84 and 123(2), (3) EPC and why the amended claims are valid (and, in a counterclaim case, why they are infringed) (RoP 30.1(b), 50.2); (c) whether the proposals are conditional; conditional proposals must be reasonable in number (RoP 30.1(c)). |
| Later requests | Only with the Court's permission (RoP 30.2; applies in the revocation action by RoP 50.2). Screen: the Application types "RoP 30.2 - Application for leave and Application to further amend a patent" and "RoP 50.2 - Application for leave and Application to further amend a patent". |
| Duty to inform | If other proceedings on the patent are pending (for example at the EPO), notify that court or authority of the application (RoP 30.3). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Application to Amend Patent" (in the Defence to revocation); in a counterclaim case also "Counterclaim defence with Amend Patent". |
| Fee | None in the 2026 table. |
| Time limit | With the defence that contains it (RoP 49.1, or RoP 29(a)). |

## Written procedure after the defence

| Filing (screen title) | Side | Rule, period, trigger | Mandatory attachment (screen label) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply to the Defence to Revocation (with any Defence to the Application to amend and any Defence to the Counterclaim for infringement) | Claimant | RoP 51: 2 months from service of the Defence to revocation; Defence to amendment: RoP 43.3, 55 with 32.1–.2 | "Statement of reply"; optional "Application to Amend Patent Defence" |
| Rejoinder to the Reply to the Defence to Revocation (with any Reply to the Defence to the Application to amend and any Reply to the Defence to the Counterclaim for infringement) | Defendant (proprietor) | RoP 52: 1 month from service of the Reply; limited to the Reply | "Statement of rejoinder" |
| Rejoinder to the reply to the application to amend a patent | Claimant | RoP 43.3, 55 with RoP 32.3: 1 month from service of the proprietor's Reply to the Defence to the Application to amend; limited to that Reply | "Statement of rejoinder" |

No fee for these filings. Further pleadings only with leave (RoP 43.5, 12.5, 36).
