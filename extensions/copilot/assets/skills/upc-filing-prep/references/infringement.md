# UPC Infringement Action and Counterclaims — Reference

**Sources:** UPC Rules of Procedure (RoP, official consolidated text, plus the rules that decision D-AC/08/02072025 replaced), UPC Agreement (UPCA), official 2026 Table of Court Fees. URLs and the check date are in `fees-2026.md`. Screen labels are the labels on the court's filing screens. **Caveat:** the RoP, the UPCA and the court's orders govern. This file is a preparation aid, not legal advice. It gives the rule, the period and the trigger event. It never gives a calendar date: service rules (RoP 270–279) and RoP 300–301 move the real date.

## Common to every filing in this family

- **Representation:** a party acts through a representative under Art. 48 UPCA (RoP 8.1).
- **Lodging and signing:** documents are signed and lodged electronically (RoP 4.1). On the screens, only a registered UPC representative can sign. One signature is enough. The signature is typed text or an image ("Signature", one JPG file).
- **Language:** the language of proceedings of the action (RoP 14). Division and language → send to `upc-division-router`.
- **Fees:** amounts only from `fees-2026.md`. Section names below point to it.
- **Deficiencies:** the Registry sends a deficiency notice. You correct it with the "Correct deficiencies" filing (see `applications-and-other-filings.md`).
- **Order of filings:** the screens offer every follow-up filing to every party of the case at any time. The law sets the side and the order. Use `next-filings.md`.

## Statement of claim (Initial Filing; Art. 32(1)(a) UPCA)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The patent proprietor; an exclusive licensee, unless the licence says otherwise, after prior notice to the proprietor; a non-exclusive licensee only if the licence expressly permits it, after prior notice (Art. 47(1)–(3) UPCA). |
| Preconditions | The patent is not opted out (the Registry checks this, RoP 16.1; Art. 83(3) UPCA) → `upc-opt-out-check`. A competent division under Art. 33 UPCA → `upc-division-router`. |
| Mandatory content (RoP 13.1) | (a) claimant name, registered office, representative; (b) defendant name and registered office; (c) claimant addresses for service and persons authorised to accept service; (d) defendant addresses for service, if known; (e) proprietor addresses, if the claimant is not the only proprietor; (f) evidence of entitlement, if the claimant is not the proprietor (Art. 47(2)–(3)); (g) patent details and number; (h) prior or pending proceedings (Court, EPO, other courts); (i) the division and why it is competent, with evidence of agreement for Art. 33(7); (j) single judge, with the defendant's agreement, if applicable; (k) the remedy sought; (l) the facts, with each infringement instance (date and place) and the claims infringed; (m) the evidence (RoP 170.1); (n) why the facts infringe, with claim interpretation; (o) orders sought in the interim procedure; (p) the value, if the claimant assesses it above EUR 500,000; (q) a list of documents, with any no-translation request and any RoP 262.2 / 262A request. Supply a copy of each document (RoP 13.2). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of claim" (mandatory, PDF); "Exhibit Claimant"; "Document Claimant"; "Proof of entitlement to 50% reduction in court fees" (if you claim the reduction); "Proof of payment" (if you select "Already paid"). |
| Other screen fields | Patent numbers (EP + 7 digits, unitary patents allowed); court division; language of proceedings; "Anticipated value of the Case" (EUR); parties: claimant, claimant representative, defendant (all mandatory), proprietor (optional); SPC data (optional). |
| Signing | A representative of the claimant. |
| Fee | Fixed fee + value-based fee (RoP 15; RoP 370.2(a), 370.3) → `fees-2026.md` §1 and §2. The Statement of claim is not lodged until the fee is paid (RoP 15.2). |
| Time limit | None to start the action. Deficiencies: correct within 14 days of service of the Registry's notice (RoP 16.3). |
| Form vs law | Law: give the value only if it exceeds EUR 500,000 (RoP 13.1(p)); the judge-rapporteur fixes it (RoP 22.1). Form: "Anticipated value of the Case" is mandatory for every action, so always enter your assessment. Law: the competence explanation is part of the pleading (RoP 13.1(i)); the form does not check Art. 33 UPCA. |

## Preliminary objection (Subsequent Filing; RoP 19)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The defendant. |
| Preconditions | A served Statement of claim. Scope only: jurisdiction and competence of the Court (including an opt-out), competence of the division, language of the Statement of claim (RoP 19.1). From a regional division, a request for transfer to the central division under Art. 33(2) UPCA (RoP 19.4). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 19.2) | Particulars of RoP 24(a)–(c); the decision or order sought; the grounds; the facts and evidence, where appropriate. Language: RoP 14 (RoP 19.3). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Preliminary objection" (mandatory); "Exhibit Defendant"; "Further pleadings"; "Document Defendant". |
| Fee | None in the 2026 table. |
| Time limit | RoP 19.1: 1 month from service of the Statement of claim. It does not stop the defence period (RoP 19.6). No objection in time = submission to the Court and the division (RoP 19.7). |

## Statement of defence (Subsequent Filing; RoP 23–24)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The defendant. |
| Mandatory content (RoP 24) | (a) defendant and representative names; (b) addresses for service; (c) the action number; (d) whether a Preliminary objection was lodged; (e) the facts, with any challenge to the claimant's facts; (f) the evidence; (g) why the action fails, including Art. 28 UPCA and claim interpretation; (h) interim orders sought; (i) whether the defendant disputes the value, with grounds; (j) list of documents and translation / confidentiality requests. RoP 13.2–.3 apply. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Statement of defence" (mandatory); "Exhibit Defendant"; "Further pleadings"; "Document Defendant". With a counterclaim, see below. |
| Screen options | Check boxes "Includes counterclaim for revocation" and "Includes counterclaim concerning licenses". Each box makes its document mandatory and adds a fee. Each counterclaim gets its own case number. |
| Fee | None without a counterclaim. |
| Time limit | RoP 23: 3 months from service of the Statement of claim. |

## Counterclaim for revocation (inside the Statement of defence; RoP 25–27)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The defendant who asserts that the patent is invalid. This assertion must be made as a counterclaim (RoP 25.1). |
| Mandatory content (RoP 25.1) | (a) extent of revocation requested; (b) grounds, with arguments of law and claim construction; (c) facts; (d) evidence; (e) interim orders sought; (f) position on the Art. 33(3) UPCA options and RoP 37.4; (g) list of documents and translation / confidentiality requests; (h) proprietor particulars (RoP 13.1(b), (d)) if the proprietor is not the claimant. |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Counterclaim for revocation" (mandatory when the box is ticked). |
| Fee | RoP 26 → `fees-2026.md` §3 (same fee as the infringement action, capped). |
| Time limit | With the Statement of defence (RoP 23, RoP 25.1). Deficiencies or unpaid fee: 14 days of service of the notice (RoP 27.2). |

## Counterclaim concerning licences (inside the Statement of defence; Art. 32(1)(a) UPCA)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The defendant in the infringement action. |
| Mandatory content | **Not verified:** the RoP have no own content rule for this counterclaim. Plead it as part of the Statement of defence (RoP 24). |
| Attachments (screen labels) | "Counterclaim concerning licenses" (mandatory when the box is ticked). |
| Fee | Fixed + value-based fee, as for an infringement action (RoP 15.3) → `fees-2026.md` §1, §2. |
| Time limit | With the Statement of defence (RoP 23). |

## Written procedure after the defence

| Filing (screen title) | Side | Rule, period, trigger | Mandatory attachment (screen label) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reply to the Statement of Defence — no counterclaim for revocation | Claimant | RoP 29(b): 2 months from service of the Statement of defence | "Statement of reply" |
| Rejoinder to the Reply to the Statement of Defence — no counterclaim for revocation | Defendant | RoP 29(c): 1 month from service of the Reply; limited to the Reply | "Statement of rejoinder" |
| Defence to the Counterclaim for revocation (with the Reply and any Application to amend the patent) | Claimant and proprietor | RoP 29(a): 2 months from service of the Statement of defence with the counterclaim. Content: RoP 29A; amendment: RoP 30 (see `revocation.md`) | "Counterclaim defence" or "Counterclaim defence with Amend Patent"; optional "Application to Amend Patent" |
| Reply to the Defence to the Counterclaim for revocation (with the Rejoinder and any Defence to the Application to amend) | Defendant | RoP 29(d): 2 months from service of the Defence to the Counterclaim; Defence to amendment: RoP 32.1 | "Counterclaim reply"; optional "Application to Amend Patent Defence" |
| Rejoinder to the Reply to the Defence to the Counterclaim for revocation (with any Reply to the Defence to the Application to amend) | Claimant and proprietor | RoP 29(e): 1 month from service of the Reply to the Defence to the Counterclaim; RoP 32.3 | "Counterclaim rejoinder" |
| Rejoinder to the reply to the application to amend a patent | Defendant | RoP 32.3: 1 month from service of the proprietor's Reply to the Defence to the Application to amend | "Statement of rejoinder" |
| Defence / Reply / Rejoinder to the Counterclaim concerning licences | Claimant / defendant / claimant | **Not verified:** no RoP rule sets these periods. Follow the judge-rapporteur's orders. | "Counterclaim defence", "Counterclaim reply", "Counterclaim rejoinder" |

No fee for these filings. Further pleadings only with leave (RoP 12.5, 36).

## Counterclaim for infringement (inside the Defence to revocation; RoP 49–56)

| Item | What applies |
|---|---|
| Who may file | The proprietor who is the defendant in a revocation action (RoP 43.2(b), 49.2(b)). |
| Mandatory content | RoP 13.1(k)–(q) (RoP 50.3); RoP 13.2–.3 apply. |
| Screen | Check box "Includes counterclaim for infringement" in the Defence to Revocation. Then "Anticipated value of the Case" and the document "Counterclaim for infringement" are mandatory. The counterclaim gets its own case number. |
| Fee | RoP 53: fixed + value-based fee → `fees-2026.md` §1, §2. |
| Time limit | With the Defence to revocation: RoP 49.1, 2 months from service of the Statement for revocation. |
| Follow-ups | Defence to the Counterclaim for infringement — revocation claimant — RoP 56.1: 2 months from service of the counterclaim; content RoP 56.2 ("Counterclaim defence"). Reply — proprietor — RoP 56.3: 1 month ("Counterclaim reply"). Rejoinder — revocation claimant — RoP 56.4: 1 month from service of the Reply ("Counterclaim rejoinder"). |

In a counterclaim case, the screens keep the party roles of the original action. Check the side from the law, not from the role names on the screen.
