# Fee-Reduction Rules — Reference

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10. Derived from a study of EPO front-office / national e-filing behaviour plus general EPC and USPTO knowledge. **Caveat:** reduction percentages, income thresholds, and eligibility criteria **drift over time and differ by office**. Everything below is an interview aid, not a fee quote — verify against the office's current official fee schedule and rules before relying on it.

## Why this is jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction

The EPO's own e-filing platform never implemented reductions centrally. Each national office layered its own incompatible logic on top, so there is no single formula — the applicant's office of filing decides. Two illustrative national patterns observed:

- ~**50%** off filing + claims fees when **every applicant is also an inventor**.
- ~**66%** off filing / search fees, gated by membership in a **reduced-fee country list** and conditioned on a **required proof attachment**.

## EPO / EPC — language-based reduction (EPC Rule 6)

| Who | Reduction (illustrative) | Condition | Proof |
|-----|--------------------------|-----------|-------|
| SMEs, natural persons, universities, non-profit / public research orgs | ~30% off filing and examination fees | File in an official language of an EPC contracting state that is NOT an EPO official language (EN/FR/DE), then supply a translation | Entity-status statement + the required translation |

## USPTO — entity-size reductions

| Status | Reduction (illustrative, most fees) | Gate |
|--------|-------------------------------------|------|
| Small entity | ~60% off | Entity qualifies as small (e.g. individual, small business, non-profit) |
| Micro entity | ~80% off | Small-entity criteria PLUS income and prior-filing limits |

Both require a certification/declaration of status; making a false certification carries consequences. Percentages are current-schedule dependent and change — do not quote them as fixed.

## Interview checklist (drives the advice)

1. Jurisdiction / office of filing.
2. Applicant type: natural person · SME · university · non-profit/research · large entity.
3. Filing language vs the office's official languages.
4. Is every applicant also a named inventor?

Map answers → candidate reductions above → name the proof each needs → repeat the drift caveat.
