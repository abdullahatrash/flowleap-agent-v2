# Excess-Claim Fee Thresholds — Reference

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10. Thresholds reflect long-standing EPO and USPTO structures; **amounts are omitted on purpose** because per-claim fee values change with every schedule revision. **Caveat:** the official EPO and USPTO fee schedules govern — confirm both the thresholds and the current per-claim amounts before quoting a number.

## Thresholds

| Office | Free up to | Charged | Rate bands |
|--------|-----------|---------|------------|
| EPO | 15 claims | Each claim from the 16th | Band A: 16th–50th (lower per-claim rate); Band B: 51st onward (higher per-claim rate) |
| USPTO | 20 total claims AND 3 independent claims | Each claim over 20 total; each independent claim over 3 | One per-claim rate for excess total; a separate (higher) rate for each excess independent claim |

Multiple-dependent claims may be counted specially (and carry their own surcharge at some offices) — flag them rather than assuming a plain count.

## Worked example — 22 claims, 4 independent

| Office | Excess total | Excess independent | Claims surcharged |
|--------|--------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| EPO | 22 − 15 = **7** | n/a | 7 (the 16th–22nd) |
| USPTO | 22 − 20 = **2** | 4 − 3 = **1** | 2 on total + 1 excess independent |

## How to present

- Count total and independent claims from the actual draft (do not assume).
- Apply the thresholds above; show the excess count per office.
- Present per-claim amounts only as illustrative reference values, each labelled "verify against the current fee schedule".
- Where merging or deleting claims would drop the count below a threshold, say how many.
