# UPC Rules of Procedure — Reference

**Verified against RoP as adopted 8 July 2022, in force 1 September 2022 (official consolidated English text), checked 2026-10-08:** https://www.unified-patent-court.org/sites/default/files/upc_documents/rop_en_25_july_2022_final_consolidated_published_on_website.pdf — later amendments (decision of 4 November 2025, in force 1 January 2026) concern court fees (e.g. RoP 370); confirm the current consolidated text before relying on a rule.

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10; rule meanings and windows corrected 2026-10-08. **Caveat:** the current UPC Rules of Procedure and the court's orders govern; treat this as a plain-language aid, not authority. Windows below are the ranges the rules contemplate, described for orientation only — they are NOT party-specific deadlines. A real deadline depends on the service date, extensions, and case-management orders and must be confirmed with counsel and the court.

## Case lifecycle (RoP 10)

Written procedure → interim procedure (may include an interim conference, RoP 101–106) → oral procedure → deliberation and decision on the merits (RoP 118). Separate procedures for the determination of damages (RoP 125 ff.) and for cost decisions (RoP 150 ff.) can follow. Evidence-preservation, provisional-measures and appeal tracks run alongside this backbone.

## The boundary this reference exists to hold

Every window above is a range the rule contemplates, for explaining what a step involves. It is not a deadline calculation. When a user wants their actual date, describe the window and route them to counsel and the court for the binding figure.
