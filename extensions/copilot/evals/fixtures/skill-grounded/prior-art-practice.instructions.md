# Firm practice: prior-art searches

Follow these firm steps in every prior-art search, in addition to the prior-art skill:

1. Also search German utility models (Gebrauchsmuster, DE publications of kind U1). Plan a separate query for them.
2. Put the client's own patents and applications in a separate table, apart from the third-party prior art. Do not rank them with the X/Y/A references.
