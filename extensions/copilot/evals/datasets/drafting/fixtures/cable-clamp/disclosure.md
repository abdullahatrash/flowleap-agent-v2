# Invention disclosure: cable clamp with a hinged lid

Invented matter for the drafting regression eval. Not a real invention.

## §1 Field

The invention relates to clamps that hold an electrical cable on a mounting rail in a control
cabinet.

## §2 Background

Known cable clamps are two loose parts that the installer screws together around the cable.
One part is easily dropped inside the cabinet, and the screw needs a tool.

## §3 Description

§3.1 The clamp has a clamp body 10 made in one piece. The clamp body 10 has a channel that
receives the cable.

§3.2 A lid 12 is joined to the clamp body 10 by a film hinge, so the lid 12 cannot be lost.
The lid 12 closes over the channel.

§3.3 A latch 14 on the free end of the lid 12 snaps behind a catch 16 on the clamp body 10.
The installer closes the clamp by hand, without a tool.

§3.4 A rail foot 18 under the clamp body 10 slides onto a standard mounting rail.

§3.5 The lid 12 is made of polyamide. Polypropylene is an alternative material for the lid 12.

## §4 Examples

§4.1 Example 1 (we ran it): we closed a batch of clamps on a cable with a diameter of 12 mm
and pulled the cable with a force of 200 N. The latch 14 of every clamp stayed closed.

§4.2 Example 2 (not yet run): we expect that a clamp with a lid of polypropylene will hold the
cable at −30 °C, because polypropylene stays flexible at that temperature.
