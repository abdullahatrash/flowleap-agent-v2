# Claims

1. A method of scheduling data synchronisation on a mobile device, the method comprising: reading a battery level of the mobile device; detecting a network type of the mobile device; and starting a synchronisation job, provided that the battery level is above a threshold and the network type is an unmetered network.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the threshold is 30 percent of a full charge.

3. The method of claim 1, further comprising placing the synchronisation job in a job queue, provided that the network type is a metered network.

4. The method of claim 1, wherein the synchronisation job is encrypted with a session key.
