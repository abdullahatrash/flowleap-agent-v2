| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | An anti-fog coating composition of a hydrophilic polymer, a crosslinker and water as the solvent. | disclosure:§3.1 |
| F2 | The crosslinker is citric acid. | disclosure:§3.1 |
| F3 | The hydrophilic polymer is polyvinyl alcohol. | disclosure:§3.1 |
| F4 | A non-ionic surfactant can be added to help the composition wet the lens. | disclosure:§3.2 |
| F5 | Applied over a primer layer 22 on a polycarbonate lens substrate 20 and cured at 120 °C to form a coating layer 24; its outer surface 26 spreads condensed water into a thin film. | disclosure:§3.3, disclosure:§3.4 |
| F6 | Working example: a coated lens over boiling water stayed clear for 60 s; an uncoated lens fogged at once. | disclosure:§4.1 |
| F7 | Prophetic example: curing at 100 °C instead of 120 °C is expected to give the same fog resistance with less heat stress on the lens substrate 20. | disclosure:§4.2 |
