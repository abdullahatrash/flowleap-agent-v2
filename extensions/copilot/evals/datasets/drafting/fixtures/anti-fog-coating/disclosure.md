# Invention disclosure: crosslinked anti-fog coating for lenses

Invented matter for the drafting regression eval. Not a real invention.

## §1 Field

The invention relates to anti-fog coatings for polycarbonate lenses.

## §2 Background

Known anti-fog coatings wash off after a few cleanings. Surfactant sprays must be applied
again after every cleaning.

## §3 Description

§3.1 The anti-fog coating composition contains polyvinyl alcohol as the hydrophilic polymer,
citric acid as the crosslinker, and water as the solvent.

§3.2 A non-ionic surfactant can be added to help the composition wet the lens.

§3.3 The composition is applied over a primer layer 22 on a lens substrate 20 of polycarbonate
and cured at 120 °C to form a coating layer 24.

§3.4 The outer surface 26 of the coating layer 24 spreads condensed water into a thin film, so
the lens stays clear.

## §4 Examples

§4.1 Example 1 (we ran it): we held a coated lens over boiling water. It stayed clear for 60 s;
an uncoated lens fogged at once.

§4.2 Example 2 (not yet run): we expect that curing at 100 °C instead of 120 °C will give the
same fog resistance with less heat stress on the lens substrate 20.
