# Claims

1. An anti-fog coating composition comprising: a hydrophilic polymer; a crosslinker; and water, wherein the crosslinker is citric acid.

2. The composition of claim 1, further comprising a non-ionic surfactant.

3. The composition of claim 1, wherein the hydrophilic polymer is polyvinyl alcohol.

4. The composition of claim 1, further comprising a silica filler.
